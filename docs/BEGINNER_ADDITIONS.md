# Beginner expansion

16 starter lessons, including four new focused practice lessons. All 16 also appear in the searchable beginner catalog. Original video IDs and completion storage remain stable; the capstone is now lesson 16.

- [Effects in After Effects - AE Fundamentals](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sM6mH20ItHY) — Optional practice after the core lessons; interface details may differ by AE version.
- [Animating Artwork from Adobe Illustrator in After Effects - AE Fundamentals](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qV3KXRrGm-A) — Optional workflow requiring Illustrator. The final Overlord segment covers a paid extension; the lesson also covers standard file import.
- [3 Ways to Export GIFs from After Effects - AE Fundamentals](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7I6xZQgVnAU) — Optional: requires Media Encoder or Photoshop for the demonstrated standard workflows. GifGun is a paid alternative, not a requirement.
- [After Effects Tutorial: SUPER EASY Grid Animation!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwNumopfYxE) — A practice project after the foundations, not a replacement for learning keyframes.
